# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Job seekers updating personal account details while browsing and applying for jobs. Employers exist in the product but are out of scope for Edit Profile for now. Context: authenticated, task-focused, often mid-job-hunt.

## Product Purpose

A job portal where seekers discover roles, apply, and keep a basic profile current so applications and account identity stay accurate. Success on Edit Profile: update name and gender quickly, with clear feedback, without leaving the authenticated app flow.

## Brand Personality

Practical, trustworthy, approachable. Clear over clever. Helpful without marketing fluff.

## Anti-references

Generic purple SaaS dashboards, dense enterprise HR systems, login-card reuse for authenticated settings, decorative glassmorphism, and template-looking Bootstrap admin skins with no brand signal.

## Design Principles

1. Task first: every authenticated screen should make the next action obvious.
2. Brand continuity: the green accent signals Job Portal across marketing and product surfaces.
3. Familiar affordances: standard forms, labels, and feedback over novel UI.
4. Honest density: enough structure for clarity, no decorative chrome.
5. Trust through clarity: validation, loading, and success states must be explicit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast. Visible focus states on all controls. Honor prefers-reduced-motion. Labels on every field (no placeholder-as-label). Keyboard-complete flows.
